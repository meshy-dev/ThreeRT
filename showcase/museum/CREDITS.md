# Sculpture Gallery (`museum`): files and credits

Everything the scene reads is in `assets/showcase/museum/` (11.4 MB together, largest file 3.2 MB). All of it is CC0;
no attribution is required, it is given here anyway. The room itself (walls, laylight, plinths, bench, frames, track
lights) is built in code.

## Poly Haven (CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license)

Each model was downloaded as glTF (`https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<id>`, the `gltf` entry at the resolution named)
with its `.bin` and textures and packed into one `.glb`; nothing else was changed. Textures are the 1k JPEGs as served.

|---|---|---|---|

| `lion_head_1k.glb` | Lion Head: bronze lion's head on a base, 47 214 triangles, 1k maps | Tina | https://polyhaven.com/a/lion_head |

| `herringbone_parquet_diff_1k.jpg`, `_nor_gl_1k.jpg`, `_rough_1k.jpg` | Herringbone Parquet: the floor | Sergej Majboroda (photography), Jenelle van Heerden (processing) | https://polyhaven.com/a/herringbone_parquet |
