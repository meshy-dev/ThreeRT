(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets). Each was downloaded as
`Models/<Name>/glTF-Binary/<Name>.glb` and repacked by `showcase/showroom/repack-assets.mjs` into
`threert-assets/showcase/showroom/` (5.5 MB together, the largest file 1.8 MB). The licence is the one in each

## Changes made to the files (CC BY asks that they are stated)

All six: textures scaled to at most 1024 px (most were 2048 px) and stored as JPEG; geometry untouched; a note of the
repack in `asset.extras.modified`.

- `shoe.glb`: `KHR_materials_variants` (not read by the engine) replaced by one node per colourway (`shoe_midnight`,
  `shoe_beach`, `shoe_street`) that share the mesh.
- `sunglasses.glb`: the ear hooks' texture, which is the Khronos Group and 3D Commerce logos (trademarks, excluded
  from the licence), replaced by a plain near-black. The two lens materials (a clear index-1 shell under an
  iridescent film, `KHR_materials_iridescence`, which the engine does not read) replaced by opaque dark polished
  glass (base colour 0.012, roughness 0.03, ior 1.5). The original's blue-violet mirror coating is therefore not shown.
- `lamp.glb`: no change besides the textures. Its `KHR_materials_iridescence` and `KHR_materials_volume` are not
  read by the engine: the glass and the inside of the shade are shown without the iridescent tint.
- `chair.glb`: `KHR_materials_variants` replaced by nodes (`chair_fabric_mango` / `chair_fabric_peacock`,
  `chair_wood_brown` / `chair_wood_black`, `chair_metal`); the paper label under the seat is left out of the scene.
  `KHR_materials_sheen` is kept in the file but not read by the engine (the velvet has no sheen lobe).
- `boombox.glb`: `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` 400 added, so that the display and lamps (emissive 1 in the
  original) read in a lit room.
- `bottle.glb`: no change besides the textures.

Everything else in the scene (the room, plinths, shelves, the terrazzo, oak and plaster textures, the street) is
generated by `showroom.game.ts`.
